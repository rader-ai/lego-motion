# Fan rules: what we can use and what stays off limits

Owner: the **Producer**, with the Creative Director checking every board and film before it leaves the studio. Source: research/07 (IP and brand rules), research/06 section 6 (AI and platform rules). This is research turned into house rules, not legal advice.

## 1. The mode we're in

Chris chose **personal and fun only** (Oct 17, 2026). That puts the studio in fan mode:
- Real brick parts from the LDraw library, including standard figure parts, are fine to build and render.
- Everything stays non-commercial: no ads, no client work, no sponsored posts, no sales of boards, prints or films.
- Sharing with family, friends and public fan spaces is fine with the credits and disclaimer below.
- Monetized channels are a gray area. The toy maker's fan policy only covers non-commercial use and says nothing about video ad revenue (research/07 section 5). Turning on ads counts as a change of mode (section 6).

## 2. Names and marks

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never use the toy maker's brand name in a repo, plugin, skill, file, folder, film title, channel or board title. Use "brick-built" or "brick". | Its fan policy bars the name in addresses and treats it as an adjective-only word mark (research/07 section 4). |
| Never show the toy maker's logo: not on boards, boxes, signs, props or studs. | The logo may never appear on unofficial material. |
| **Studs never carry the toy maker's logo.** Turn off logo studs in the LDraw importer and check every render. Studs carry our own house glyph in close-ups, or stay blank on wide shots (LOOK_DEV.md section 3). | The logo on studs is protected; plain studs are generic (research/07 section 1). Blank studs look wrong up close (research/05 section 2). |
| Use a house mark of our own on Set boards and box-style layouts. | Box art without the logo needs something in that spot. Chris approves the mark. |
| Say "figure", never the trademarked figure name, in studio text and titles. | The figure name is on the toy maker's trademark list. |
| Where a description names the toy maker at all, add: "Not sponsored, authorized or endorsed by the LEGO Group." | The fan policy asks for a disclaimer wherever the word appears. |

## 3. Characters, sets and stories

- **Original only.** Every character, face print, torso print, world, vehicle and story is ours. This rule is also in the clay studio and is the same reason: we want work that's ours.
- **No film characters, no licensed themes.** Nothing from the brick films, and nothing from franchises sold as licensed sets (space sagas, superheroes, wizards, and so on). A character built from bricks is still that character (research/07 section 3).
- **No copies of official sets, instruction booklets or box art.** We make our own Set boards and instruction sheets in our own layouts. The toy maker owns copyright in its instructions and catalog photos.
- **Family and friends can be figures** with their OK, built from generic cues (hair color, glasses, a jacket, one prop), never a claimed likeness. No public figures.
- **Making text stays clean.** Skill and agent text that drives making cites research file numbers, not real studio, film, set or character names (same rule as the clay studio).

## 4. Credits on everything that leaves the studio

- "Brick parts from the LDraw Parts Library (ldraw.org), CC BY 2.0." The LDraw Steering Committee says renders aren't derivative works, but crediting costs nothing. Anything that ships converted part meshes (an exported .blend or .glb) needs this credit (research/07 section 6).
- The disclaimer in section 2 when the toy maker is named.
- "Made with AI assistance" on any board or film where a generated image, video or voice is visible or audible. Brick fan communities react strongly to unlabeled AI (research/06 section 6).

## 5. Tools and their terms

- **LDraw parts library:** CC BY 2.0, fine for this. The All-In-One installer is CC BY-ND, so install the parts zip, not the installer.
- **BrickLink Studio and Mecabricks:** their terms for render use weren't readable (research/07 section 6). Use them only to look things up or build by hand for reference; production builds and renders go through LDraw files and Blender.
- **Rebrickable data** (part and color availability): free for any use, credit appreciated.

## 6. If Chris ever wants commercial use

That's a new decision at a gate, not a drift. What changes (research/07 section 8):
1. An original figure that doesn't share the protected combination (barrel head with top stud, trapezoid torso, C-hook hands, hip block with leg holes). How different is enough is untested; counsel decides.
2. Original names for the figure type, parts, themes and colors.
3. Every element checked against newer part designs.
4. Counsel answers the ranked questions in research/07 section 9 first.
Until then, fan mode holds.
